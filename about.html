Our Founding Story

How Link Lanka Transformed Sri Lanka’s Taxi Sector

In 2008, Sri Lanka’s taxi and three-wheeler sector lacked reliable, tamper-proof metering. Imported meters were prohibitively expensive, delicate, and lacked spare parts. Entrepreneur Thushara Kariyakarawana recognized the opportunity to manufacture locally.

By teaming with Japanese electronics manufacturing experts in the Biyagama FTZ, Link Lanka engineered a robust digital meter that could handle tropical humidity, bumpy roads, and daily continuous operation. The product became a nationwide sensation, earning over 90% market adoption.
