Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How are tariff rate changes and vehicle configurations secured?

Link Lanka meters eliminate fragile physical lead wire seals by implementing advanced digitally secured rate change and vehicle model configuration protocols. All tariff updates and vehicle calibrations are protected cryptographically, ensuring tamper-proof integrity and complete compliance.

Where can I purchase genuine thermal printer paper rolls?

Standard 57mm high-contrast thermal paper rolls certified for Link Lanka printers are available in single rolls or bulk packs at all 9 service yards and through authorized retail points nationwide.

What is the warranty period on a new Link Taxi Meter?

All Link Lanka brand digital taxi meters come with a comprehensive 1-year manufacturer warranty covering circuit boards, pulse sensor, and print mechanism against manufacturing defects.
