Key Model Assumptions (150 KM / Day)

  • Fuel Efficiency: 20–25 km per Litre on commercial three-wheelers.
  • Deadheading: 50 km empty running factored per 100 km passenger hire.
  • Driver Wage: Rs. 3,000 baseline fair daily earnings reserve.
